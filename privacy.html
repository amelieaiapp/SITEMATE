Privacy Policy

AMELIE·AI PRO-PLUS — Site Mate · last updated 27 July 2026

The short version: Site Mate works offline and keeps your data on your own device. We do not collect it, we do not upload it, and we do not sell it. There are no adverts, no tracking, and no analytics.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the AMELIE·AI PRO-PLUS Site Mate application ("the app"), published on the Microsoft Store.

2. What data the app stores, and where

Everything you enter into Site Mate — your programme, site diary, records, photos, tasks, settings and any other content — is stored locally on your device only, using the device's own storage. It is your data. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export or share it yourself (for example, saving a report, or sending a hit list by WhatsApp or email using your device's own apps).

We, the developer, have no access to this data. We cannot see it, retrieve it, or recover it. You can back it up or export all of it at any time from within the app, and you can delete it by clearing the app's data.

3. What the app does not do

Site Mate does not create an account, does not require a login, and does not send your site data to us or to any third party. It contains no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no usage analytics.

4. Limited internet use (only when you choose it)

Site Mate is designed to run fully offline. It connects to the internet in only two optional situations:

  • Weather forecast (optional): if you use the weather feature, the app requests a forecast from a public weather service (Open-Meteo). This sends only an approximate location and the dates needed for the forecast. No personal or site data is sent.
  • AI narration — bring-your-own-key (optional, off by default): the app can optionally use an AI service that you connect with your own API key (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI). Data is transmitted to that service only when you have deliberately entered your own key and pressed "Narrate". If you never do this, nothing is sent. Administrators can switch AI off entirely and demonstrate that it is isolated. When you use this feature, your use is subject to the privacy terms of whichever AI provider's key you supplied.

On first load, the app also fetches its fonts and icons from the web; after that it runs offline.

5. Purchases and subscription

Buying the app, the free trial, and the subscription are handled by the Microsoft Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Microsoft's handling of that information is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

6. Children

Site Mate is a professional construction tool intended for use by adults in the workplace. It is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
